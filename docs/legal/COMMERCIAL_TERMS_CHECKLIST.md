# Commercial terms checklist

Source licenses do not replace the contracts needed to operate an AI company.

## Public website and consumer product

- Terms of Service
- Privacy Policy
- Usage/Acceptable Use Policy
- cookie and analytics disclosures where applicable
- subscription, renewal, cancellation, refund, and tax terms
- warranty, limitation-of-liability, and dispute terms reviewed for target
  jurisdictions
- age and supported-region rules

## API and developer platform

- API/service terms
- account, credential, and key-security rules
- rate limits, quotas, suspension, and abuse response
- input/output ownership and license terms
- training/data-use policy
- retention and deletion behavior
- model/service changes and deprecation notice
- prohibited reverse engineering, credential resale, and circumvention
- usage metering, billing, credits, and taxes

## Enterprise and self-hosted products

- master service agreement and order form
- commercial software license/EULA outside PolyForm Shield
- support and service-level schedule
- data processing addendum and security exhibit
- subprocessor list
- confidentiality and audit terms
- deployment scope, seats/nodes/usage metrics, and affiliates
- updates, end-of-life, backup, recovery, and incident notification
- warranties, indemnities, liability caps, and insurance requirements
- termination, data export, transition, and destruction

## Research and early access

- evaluation/preview agreement
- confidentiality and publication-review terms
- benchmark and attribution rules
- feedback license
- no-production and no-reliance boundaries where appropriate
- safety reporting and access termination

## Operational rule

Do not paste these headings into a homemade contract and call it complete.
Use them to scope counsel's work and ensure product architecture, sales promises,
privacy behavior, and actual system operation agree.
