# Contributing to Ghost

Ghost is not accepting external pull requests at this pre-company boundary.
Issues and technical discussion are welcome, but do not submit code, art,
models, datasets, documentation, or other copyrightable material for inclusion
unless the maintainer has explicitly invited it under written terms.

This pause prevents ambiguous ownership while Canticle is formed and its
founder-owned intellectual property is assigned. Before external contributions
are accepted, Canticle intends to publish:

- a contributor agreement that preserves provenance and the ability to offer
  commercial licenses;
- a contribution workflow with authorship and third-party-origin declarations;
- security reporting and code-of-conduct routes; and
- the exact company identity authorized to accept contributions.

An issue report does not transfer ownership of an implementation. Do not post
secrets, customer data, private repository contents, private model artifacts,
or material you do not have permission to disclose.
