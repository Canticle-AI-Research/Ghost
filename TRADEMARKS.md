# Canticle trademark policy

This is an interim policy for the Canticle, Ghost, SEAM, MIRL, Canticle Core,
and SEAM-U names, logos, mascots, and trade dress (the **Canticle marks**).
Until a company is formed and the relevant rights are assigned, Nicholas
Thomas is the identified owner of these marks and associated goodwill.

The software license does not grant trademark rights. Subject to applicable
law, you may:

- truthfully state that your software uses or is compatible with an official
  Canticle product;
- reproduce an unmodified Canticle mark only when necessary for that truthful
  reference; and
- retain required copyright and attribution notices.

Without separate written permission, you may not:

- name a company, product, service, domain, package, or social account in a way
  that suggests it is official or endorsed by Canticle;
- use the Canticle marks to brand modified forks or competing products;
- alter logos, mascots, or trade dress and present them as official; or
- use the marks in deceptive, defamatory, or unlawful material.

Forks should use clearly independent names and branding. Nominative references
should be no more prominent than needed to describe compatibility.

This engineering policy is not a trademark registration, legal opinion, or a
waiver of rights. It should be reviewed by trademark counsel and updated to the
company's exact legal name after formation, assignment, and authorization.
